Good to know

Straight answers, before you commit.

Everything is discussed openly before a project starts.

How much does a website cost?

Projects are scoped around pages, content and functionality, and premium projects typically start from £2,500. You receive a written scope and quote before you decide anything.

Will a new website bring me more clients?

A better website sharpens your presentation, makes your offer clearer and makes enquiring easier. Results also depend on traffic, pricing, sales follow-up and the market, so we never promise specific numbers. The ROI Lab is an illustration, not a forecast.

Do you offer monthly website care?

It is optional. If ongoing support is useful, we agree exactly what is included, how requests are handled, and the fee before it begins. It is not a mandatory subscription.

Do you offer paid ads or software?

Not yet. We do not run paid ad campaigns or build custom software, and we only list what we can deliver well. Alongside the website, we do offer local search setup and enquiry automation, so more of your visitors turn into enquiries.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the number of pages, how ready your content is and how quickly feedback comes back. Your written quote includes a timeline that is agreed before you commit.

Will my website work on mobile and be search-friendly?

Yes. Every site is built to be responsive, with clean page structure, proper headings, meta details and fast-loading pages so search engines can understand it. Rankings still depend on competition, content and time, so they cannot be guaranteed.

Where are you based, and how does working across time zones work?

TRTL MEDIA is based in Maharashtra, India, and works with UK and international clients. India is 4.5 to 5.5 hours ahead of the UK depending on the season. We reply to every enquiry within one working day, and everything is written down by email, so nothing depends on being online at the same time.

Can I update the website myself after launch?

We agree this before the build. Either we make updates for you, quoted separately or under the optional care plan, or we set up a simple way for you to edit key content. You will know which before you commit.
