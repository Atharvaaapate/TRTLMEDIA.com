Good to know

Questions about pricing.

The details are discussed openly before a project starts.

Why do prices start from a figure?

Every project is different. The final fee depends on the pages, content, functionality and requirements involved, and is confirmed in your written quote before you commit.

What is not included?

Domains, hosting, premium fonts, stock media and paid plugins are not included unless your quote says so. Content changes after launch are quoted separately, or covered by the optional website care plan.

Are prices in pounds, and do they include tax?

Prices are shown in pounds sterling. They exclude any VAT, GST or other taxes that may apply, which are set out in your quote.

How does payment work?

The payment schedule is set out in your written quote, and a project starts once you accept it in writing and any agreed advance is received.

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes. Redesign is quoted in the same way, based on the pages and requirements involved.

Can a website guarantee more enquiries?

No. A website can improve presentation, clarity and the path to enquiry, but results also depend on traffic, pricing, sales follow-up and the market.
